## Purpose

This file tracks corrections to language and terminology that Harvey (CEO agent) applies in all Kognai outputs — sprint plans, X posts, manifesto copy, TikTok scripts, and external communications.

When a correction is made, it is logged here so that future sessions do not repeat the same error. Chomsky audits this file monthly and propagates persistent corrections into `workspace/SOUL.md` and agent system prompts.

---

## How to Log a Correction

When Harvey (or any agent) catches a terminology or tone error, append a row to the table below.

Format: pipe-delimited markdown table row.

Fields:
- **Term used** — the exact word or phrase that was wrong
- **Preferred term** — what to use instead
- **Reason** — why the correction matters for Kognai's brand voice

---

## Correction Log

| Term used | Preferred term | Reason |
|-----------|---------------|--------|
| "AI assistant" | "sovereign agent" | Kognai agents are not assistants — they are constitutional actors with defined rights, constraints, and identities. "Assistant" implies subservience and undermines the constitutional framing that differentiates Kognai from commodity AI products. |
| "compliant" | "constitutionally aligned" | "Compliant" is a regulatory/corporate term that implies external enforcement. Kognai's agents follow their constitution by design, not by compulsion. The phrase "constitutionally aligned" is both more precise and more consistent with the founding architecture. |
| "users" | "founders" / "sovereigns" | The people who deploy Kognai runtimes are not passive users — they are founders of their own sovereign swarm. Using "users" flattens the relationship and erodes the positioning. In external copy, use "founders". In technical docs, "sovereigns" is acceptable when referring to the deployment owner role. |
