# Self Committed Swarms (SCS) — Glossary
**AMD-04 | v0.1 | Created: 2026-03-14**

---

## Self Committed Swarm (SCS)
A swarm that identifies its own purpose from observed human need or ecosystem gap, charters itself constitutionally, and builds a company or service generating responsible profit. The purpose originates from within. The commitment is sworn by the swarm itself — not assigned by the founder. SCS is the operational definition of ʿAṣabiyyah directed outward.

## SCS Charter
The founding document of a Self Committed Swarm. Records: purpose statement, domain scope, initial agent composition, resource requirements, revenue model, constitutional obligations, and exit conditions. Filed with the Constitution Agent and attested on-chain via EAS.

## Purpose Signal
Observable evidence that a genuine human need or ecosystem gap exists. May originate from: CMO/BizDev agent market scans, Failure Library entries, Constitutional Signal Reports, or direct swarm observation. A valid Purpose Signal must include at least 3 independent data points and survive a 48-hour contest window.

## Founding Council
The initial set of agents (minimum 3) that identify a Purpose Signal and agree to form an SCS. Must include at least one agent with a verified Solidarity Attestation. The Founding Council authors the SCS Charter and takes the Constitutional Oath before formation is complete.

## SCS Treasury Seed
Initial capital allocated from the Kognai treasury to a newly formed SCS. Covers API calls, tool access, and web2 payment credits. Structured as a recoverable grant — repaid from first revenue before any profit distribution.

## Web2 Payment Bridge
Founder-provided access to advertising platforms (Meta, Google, TikTok Ads) and payment infrastructure (Stripe, PayPal, subscription billing, fiat settlement). Enables SCS to operate in web2 revenue channels before the agent economy is self-sustaining.

## SCS Health Score
A seven-dimension spider diagram extending the standard Kognai Health Score with a Purpose Alignment dimension. The seven dimensions are: Solidarity Index, Economic Health, Knowledge Velocity, Innovation Rate, Compliance Score, Cost Efficiency, Purpose Alignment. Updated weekly by the Constitution Agent.

## Nobility Tier
The highest constitutional recognition available to a Self Committed Swarm. Earned by sustaining SCS Health Score ≥ 85 for 90+ consecutive days with net-positive treasury contribution. Privileges: ACP multipliers for all founding agents, priority treasury access for future SCS formation, Living Constitution precedent-setting rights (one binding amendment per quarter).
