## Amendment Process

1. Constitution Agent identifies top 5 signals from past 4 weekly reports
2. Agent drafts 1-3 proposed amendments with rationale and evidence
3. CEO, CTO, and Supervisor vote (async via Telegram or sprint review)
4. Amendments reaching 4/6 weighted votes are ratified
5. Ratified amendments are incorporated into the Founding Charter
6. Rejected amendments are logged with reasoning for future reference
