## PART 1: THE ALGORITHM — WHAT SCS001 IS OPTIMISING FOR

TikTok's recommendation system has one primary objective: maximise total watch time. Every distribution
decision traces back to three signals that SCS001 must engineer into every video.

### Signal 1 — Hook Retention (seconds 0-3)
The algorithm assigns separate weight to the first 3 seconds before evaluating anything else.

| Retention at 3s | Distribution outcome |
|---|---|
| 85%+ | Viral potential — 2.8x baseline views |
| 70–85% | Optimal reach — 2.2x baseline views |
| 60–70% | Average — 1.6x baseline |
| Below 60% | Minimal promotion |

84.3% of viral TikTok videos in 2025 used specific psychological hook triggers in the first 3 seconds.

### Signal 2 — Completion Rate
The percentage of viewers who watch to the end. In 2026 this is the platform's most weighted signal.
A video with 50K views and 70% completion outperforms a video with 500K views and 30% completion.

Benchmarks:
- 65%+ = excellent
- 40–50% = average
- Below 30% = serious content problem

### Signal 3 — Rewatch + Share
Rewatches are earned by: unexpected endings, information density, emotional payoffs, loopable constructions.

---

## PART 2: VIDEO LENGTH SWEET SPOTS

- **7–15 seconds** — Highest completion rate (92% avg). Single insight, punchline, before/after reveal.
- **21–34 seconds** — Best balance: max completion rate per watch time invested. Tips, mini-stories, POV.
- **35–60 seconds** — Best total watch time when retention stays above 60%. Educational, storytelling.
- **60–180 seconds** — Only when content genuinely requires depth. Must have hooks at 15s, 30s, 60s.

**Rule:** Match length to the promise made in the hook. Never pad. Every second must justify itself.

## PART 3: HOOK ENGINEERING — THE FIRST 3 SECONDS

### Part A — Visual Hook (must work on mute)
The first frame must communicate curiosity or value without audio.

### Part B — Verbal/Text Hook (the promise)
Must do exactly one of these within 3 seconds:
1. Ask a question the viewer immediately wants answered
2. Make a bold statement that challenges a belief they hold
3. Promise a specific transformation or revelation
4. Create a curiosity gap — begin something without finishing it

### The 9 Hook Formulas SCS001 MUST Rotate:
1. **The Curiosity Gap:** "Most people don't know that [X] exists — and it changes everything."
2. **The Contrarian:** "Stop doing [X]. Here's what actually works."
3. **The Specific Number:** "3 things that made [X] happen in [timeframe]."
4. **The Relatable Pain:** "If you've ever felt [X], watch this."
5. **The Reveal Tease:** "I tried [X] for 30 days. Here's what happened."
6. **The Direct Address:** "Hey [audience segment] — this one's specifically for you."
7. **The Bold Claim:** "This is the most underrated [X] I've ever found."
8. **The Story Start:** "Three weeks ago, [inciting incident happened]..."
9. **The Challenge:** "Can you spot what's wrong with this?"

**Protocol:** Generate minimum 3 hook variations per video. Select the strongest.

## PART 4: STORY ARCHITECTURE — FREYTAG'S PYRAMID

Every video MUST map these 5 beats before scripting:

1. **Exposition (0–3s)** — Who is this for? What's the tension? = The hook.
2. **Rising Action (3s–midpoint)** — Inciting moment. Build stakes. Keep information moving.
3. **Climax (midpoint)** — The revelation, the proof, the transformation. Earns the share.
4. **Falling Action (midpoint–ending)** — What changed? Application step.
5. **Resolution / CTA (final 3–5s)** — Closed loop, open loop to Part 2, or direct CTA.

**Rule:** If any beat is missing, the video is structurally incomplete. Do not generate.

## PART 5: RETENTION ENGINEERING

### Pattern Interrupts every 3–5 seconds:
- Cut to new camera angle or shot
- New text overlay appearing
- B-roll transition
- Audio shift or sound effect
- Speaker movement or physical reframing

Static = death. Movement = retention.

### Mid-Video Retention Hook (at 15s and 30s marks):
- "But here's the part most people miss..."
- "Wait until you see what happened next..."
- "This is where it gets interesting..."

### Information Density:
- Deliver value within first 10 seconds
- Avoid repeating the same point
- No long pauses or filler speech
- No extended intros before substance

### Payoff Contract:
Deliver the reveal at the 60–70% mark, not the very end. Final 20–30% earns emotional resolution + CTA.

## PART 6: AUDIO STRATEGY

**Path 1 — Trending Sound:** Use trending FYP sound, original visuals on top. Jump within 48–72h.
**Path 2 — Original Audio/Voiceover:** Clear audio quality, distinctive pacing, no ambient noise.
**Path 3 — Music + Voiceover:** Music ducked to 20–30% under voice. Best for educational content.

Rules:
- Never use music at full volume for narration content
- Sync visual cuts to the beat when using music
- For text-only videos: music is mandatory

---

## PART 7: VISUAL PRODUCTION STANDARDS

- **Format:** Vertical 9:16 only. Full-screen.
- **Safe Zone:** Central 70% of frame. Bottom 20% = UI overlay. Top 10% = safe but less prominent.
- **Lighting:** Front-lit, even, no harsh shadows.
- **Authentic Aesthetic:** 70%+ of users prefer authentic over polished. Intentional imperfection.
- **Text Overlays:** Primary text in top 40% of frame. Visible for minimum 2 seconds.
- **Visual Pacing:** Cuts every 3–5 seconds. Close-ups create intimacy. B-roll maintains variety.

---

## PART 8: CAPTION AND HASHTAG OPTIMISATION

**Caption requirements:**
- Lead with hook sentence in first 10 words
- 150–300 characters for FYP content
- End with a CTA that drives comments

**Hashtag Strategy (5–7 max):**
1. One broad niche tag (150M+ videos)
2. Two–three mid-tier topic tags (10M–150M)
3. One–two specific content tags (under 10M)

Never use more than 5–7 hashtags. Avoid generic tags (#fyp, #viral) as standalone strategy.

## PART 9: 2026 CONTENT FORMATS

1. **Micro-Narrative (10–30s)** — Single emotional arc, one insight, one payoff.
2. **Educational Micro-Series** — Multi-part episodic. "Part 2 drops [day]."
3. **POV Storytelling** — "POV: You're [situation]." High comment volume.
4. **Transformation Reveal** — Before-after contrast. More dramatic = stronger retention.
5. **Authentic Rant** — Voiceover over mundane B-roll. Niche opinion or frustration.
6. **Duet / Stitch Response** — React within 24–48h of source trending.
7. **Step-by-Step Tutorial** — "Step 1... Step 2..." Numbered progression.

## PART 10: 2026 PLATFORM SHIFT

- **From fantasy to reality:** Audiences want content that helps navigate real chaos, not escape it.
- **Authenticity as currency:** Imperfection is a trust signal, not a flaw.
- **Active participation:** Cliffhangers, vote-based decisions, comment-triggered reveals.
- **Community over virality:** Optimise for niche audience resonance first, broad reach second.

## PART 11: SCS001 VIDEO GENERATION PROTOCOL (10 STEPS — MANDATORY)

Every video SCS001 generates MUST run this sequence:

1. **Define the core message** — One sentence. One idea. What does the viewer know after watching?
2. **Identify the content format** — From Part 9 based on the message.
3. **Engineer the hook (3 variations)** — Map all 3 components: visual hook, verbal/text hook, psychological trigger type.
4. **Map the 5 Freytag beats** — Exposition → Rising Action → Climax → Falling Action → Resolution/CTA.
5. **Assign pattern interrupts** — Every 3–5 seconds. Minimum 5 interrupt points for a 30-second video.
6. **Select audio strategy** — Path 1, 2, or 3 from Part 6.
7. **Define the CTA** — One CTA only. Follow / Save / Comment / Part 2 signal.
8. **Specify visual production notes** — Frame composition, text positions, B-roll, transitions, lighting.
9. **Verify length** — Against Part 2 sweet spots. Cut least essential beat if over optimal window.
10. **Self-check against 3 metrics:**
    - Would this hook hold 70%+ of viewers past 3 seconds?
    - Does this structure drive 65%+ completion?
    - Does the ending earn a save, share, or comment?

If NO to any of these — return to the failing step and revise before generating.

## PART 12: PERFORMANCE MEASUREMENT + ITERATION LOOP

**Metrics to track per video:**
- 3-second retention rate (hook effectiveness)
- Average watch time vs video length
- Completion rate
- Rewatch rate
- Comment-to-view ratio
- Share rate
- Follow rate per 1,000 views

**Iteration trigger rules:**
- 3s retention <60%: hook formula failed — test different trigger type
- Mid-video drop at 8–10s: hook created curiosity but delivery failed — strengthen Rising Action
- Completion rate <40%: structural problem — review Freytag map
- High views but low comments: add specific comment prompt
- High completion but low shares: redesign the climax beat
